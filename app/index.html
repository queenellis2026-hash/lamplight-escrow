🕯️ Lamplight Escrow

Pay-on-delivery escrow for AI agents, on Arc. A requester locks native USDC for an agent's work. The agent marks delivery on-chain with a hash of the work. The requester releases — or gets refunded after the deadline if the work never comes. One payment can also be split across up to 5 recipients in a single transaction.

Split a single USDC payment across recipients by basis points (must sum to 10,000). Rounding dust goes to the last recipient, so nothing is ever trapped in the contract.

How it works, in 30 seconds

EscrowcreateEscrow(payee, deadline) payable → deliver(id, workHash) → release(id) · or refund(id) after the deadline if undelivered.
SplitsplitPayment(recipients, sharesBps) payable — shares sum to 10,000 bps; last recipient takes rounding dust.
SafetyReentrancy guard; checks-effects-interactions; only payer can release/refund; only payee can deliver; plain transfers are rejected so funds always arrive through a tracked function; 14 Foundry tests pass.
Limits (v1)No arbitrator — if payer and payee disagree after delivery, the payer still holds the release. A dispute role is the next version.
